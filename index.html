Hi, I am Dheeraj (dragxsy)

I work in visuals across music, brands, and apparel.

My creative work spans cover arts, poster designs, music videos, promotional edits, and thumbnails. For major artworks and core visual direction I work primarily in Photoshop and for rapid layouts, editorial assets, and visual structures I utilize Canva . Visually, my work operates between disciplined composition and raw energy—driven by tactile textures, analog distortion, and deliberate artistic experimentation.
